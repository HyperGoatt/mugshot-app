# Mugshot native completion review

Technical review of the implemented native interaction, using captured local
fixtures. SwiftUI is the editable animation source; this HTML only presents the
capture and timing notes. No replacement mascot, rendered in-app interaction,
fictional product capability, or exported private journal content.

- Ground: Mugshot cream `#FAF6F0`.
- Ink: Mugshot espresso `#1F1712`.
- Accent: Mugshot mint `#A8CDB8`; readable sage `#4D6F5D`.
- Display: retained EB Garamond 700; reading: retained Inter 400, both OFL.
- Native capture keeps its original aspect ratio and colors.
- No film grain, blur, gradients over the UI, glow, dramatic camera moves,
  gamification, vendor marks, sound effects standing in for haptics, or music.
