# Proposed interaction and content contract

Approval scope: Week in review's visual/interaction replacement, its Journal entry,
the private weekly notification invitation and its share preview. This is an
isolated proposal. It does not replace root tabs, publishing, month/year summaries,
notification eligibility or privacy rules without the later implementation review.

## A story, chosen from real memories

| Chapter | What earns its place | Visual payoff | Fallback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Cover | A completed week owned by the current account | Photos fan out around a delighted native Mugsy | Calm shorter cover for one or zero memories |
| Moments | Canonical sips plus completed, non-duplicate private Home saves | Actual photos, remembered dates and one honest total | No empty-day holes or pressure to log more |
| Standout | Highest recorded overall rating; deterministic tie break | Large photo, score and optional tap-to-turn saved caption | Photo-led memory without a rating superlative; no-photo native Mugsy |
| Places | Distinct saved cafe IDs and actual Home count | Cafe tickets and a cozy Home acknowledgment | Omit chapter when there is no meaningful variation |
| Repeat order | A normalized recorded drink repeated at least twice | Two dated keepsakes and a warm recognition | Omit when there is no repeat; never invent a preference |
| Keepsake | The actual visible story | A quiet postcard and Mugsy's closing acknowledgment | Shorter close, no fabricated stats |

These are editorial choices derived from recorded facts, not an AI personality or
flavor judgment. Unknown data stays unknown. Changed/removed/private media retains
its real availability state; a failed photo is never replaced with invented history.
The weekly interval retains the existing account-bound Sunday 6 PM / UTC 168-hour
contract, with exclusive end. Historical windows must remain attached to their
original occurrence endpoint rather than changing when a later week is opened.

## Navigation and animation

The owner advances when ready. Six progress segments show the current chapter,
not points or a consumption goal. Previous, Next and Close work from the first
frame. Horizontal swipes mirror the buttons. No auto-advance, completion lock or
minimum viewing time. A chapter may be skipped by its data rule.

Motion gives each chapter a distinct gesture: photo fan, recollection collage,
reversible photo turn, collected cafe tickets, repeat-order pairing, postcard
settle. Mugsy's approved rig supplies eyes, gaze, blink, arms and planted-foot bend;
no new silhouette, palette, glasses or handle orientation. All movement is finite.
A revisit/resume should show settled content; it must not replay a save-success
celebration or present a completed-operation claim.

Reduced Motion uses settled pictures, direct chapter changes and a readable
caption alternative. Decorative Mugsy/photo layers are hidden from VoiceOver;
meaningful photos and the caption turn expose named accessible controls. Large
text can scroll inside a chapter while navigation stays reachable. Final device
accessibility/performance acceptance follows design approval and integration.

## Privacy and notification proposal

Push title: **Your Weekly Pour is ready.**

Push body: **A little coffee. A few good memories. Come take a look back with Mugsy.**

The copy is deliberately generic: no activity count, cafe, drink, private caption,
notes or photo. Existing opt-in, Sunday 6 PM schedule, completed-sip eligibility,
deduplication and account-bound routes remain. A quiet week is available in-app;
this proposal does not add an empty-week notification or increase push frequency.
No push is actually sent by the prototype.

The in-app recap is private. Sharing opens a separate explicit preview. The sample
card defaults to three non-private moments and two cafes; opting into private Home
counts changes the total to five. The card excludes private notes/captions either
way. The prototype's Share image action explains that system sharing follows
approval and sends nothing. In real integration, use the established sharing
boundary and recheck account/owned presentation before presenting the system sheet.

## Approval boundaries

Approve the proposed look, story order, tone, Mugsy staging, interaction, entry and
share/privacy presentation. Approval is followed by production data binding,
focused ownership/date/duplicate/media tests and relevant native acceptance.
Mockup rendering, an installed fixture or a sample story never proves a real
notification was delivered, the owner had these sips, or the design shipped.
