# Mugshot Full-Lifecycle Phases 0–6 Checkpoint

## Program outcome

The audited Phase 0–6 lifecycle is implemented on `codex/full-lifecycle-roadmap`. Mugshot now has a reliable Guided sipping loop, low-friction first-session activation, a canonical Journal, an explainable Taste Graph, a restrained friends layer, memory-led reflections, durable ownership controls, and shared system entry points. Phase 7 merchant rewards, payments, loyalty, and partnerships remain unimplemented future work.

The original phase gates were applied as risk-informed recommendations. Privacy leakage, data loss, broken authentication, and unsafe database changes remained hard blockers; device-matrix polish and rare presentation outliers were recorded as follow-up rather than allowed to stall useful progress.

## Final acceptance evidence

- The complete Swift unit suite passes on iPhone 17 Pro, iOS 26.2.
- The app and embedded widget build and launch from the Phase 6 branch.
- Supabase phase migrations are additive; phase contracts cover RLS, grants, actor ownership, private-note separation, retry idempotency, and export isolation.
- Guided capture is the normal experience; Long Form remains a DEBUG rollback control.
- Private notes remain structurally separate from social captions throughout application, database, Feed, analysis, export, and system-entry paths.
- Journal remains valuable without social activity, while friends features have no follower counts, popularity rankings, or consumption leaderboards.
- Taste Identity and recommendations expose their evidence and support owner correction.
- Reflections reward memory, exploration, and learning; caffeine is estimated deep data with coverage, not a goal or medical measurement.
- Guest use, authentication interruption, offline drafts, upload recovery, and authenticated publication were exercised during the phase checkpoints.
- Settings, export, cloud-health transparency, account deletion, App Intents, widgets, shortcuts, and deep links converge on existing owner and composer contracts.
- Dynamic Type, Increase Contrast, Reduce Motion, accessibility labels, focusable controls, and scroll reachability were exercised in Simulator.
- Product scans pass the ASCII `cafe`/`cafes` rule and find no new pressure-based retention copy.
- No merchant, payment, loyalty, or partnership domain was added.

## Known recommendations

- Run final VoiceOver and widget/App Shortcut gallery QA on signed physical devices before App Store distribution.
- Use a disposable owner for destructive account-deletion QA.
- Continue screenshot regression coverage across additional supported device sizes as release candidates stabilize.
- Retain phase controls for one release cycle, then remove obsolete DEBUG paths in a dedicated cleanup change.

## Release decision

The branch is suitable for user testing and final review. No known issue in the checkpoint classifies as a privacy, data-loss, authentication, or migration-safety blocker. It remains local and unpushed until explicit approval.
