# Mugshot launch quality audit

This is the release ledger for the **next source candidate**, not an acceptance of TestFlight 0.5.3 (8). All visible iPhone features stay in launch scope. Feature additions are frozen after Home Sip V3 except changes needed to make an existing journey understandable, usable, recoverable, or safe. No launch date overrides the gates below.

## Evidence and status rules

- Record every finding as `LQ-NNN`, with expected and observed behavior, exact build/account, steps, severity, owner, repair, and closure proof. A source change is **implemented**; a test or screenshot is **locally verified**; a deployed backend change is **production-configured**; hardware and a processed TestFlight build require their own acceptance.
- The 2026-09-25 screenshots in [the evidence set](audits/launch-quality-2026-09-25/screens/) came from a clean iPhone 17 Pro iOS 27 Simulator. Screens 01–04 used the ordinary first-run route. Screens 05–13 used `--ui-testing --ui-testing-reset --ui-testing-reduce-motion` and synthetic local data. Those fixture screens cannot prove authentication, production networking, or persistence. No production user content was written.
- On iOS 27, `MugshotOnboardingUITests.testFirstLaunchSkipJumpsToAccountChoice` passed under the `MugshotTests` scheme with the guest choice assertion. The app scheme contains no test bundles and its attempted `test_sim` run collected zero tests; that run is not counted as a test failure or acceptance.
- A clean iPhone 16 Pro iOS 18.6 Simulator (the nearest installed runtime to the 18.5 deployment target) built and launched the current source; first-run Skip exposed the [revised account choice](audits/launch-quality-2026-09-25/screens/18-account-choice-iphone-only.jpg), and the guest choice opened its introduction and Map. iOS 18.5 itself and signed hardware remain untested.
- The source branch is `codex/launch-quality-audit` from main commit `8cc8472`, ahead of distributed 0.5.3 (8). The initial screenshots predate the later import/share refinements and are discovery evidence, not exact-candidate acceptance. Assign a version/build and commit SHA to the consolidated candidate before rerunning the matrix. The build number shown in project settings is still 8; no new TestFlight artifact exists.
- A state is **Open** until its stated closure evidence is recorded here. The [TestFlight feedback ledger](TESTFLIGHT_FEEDBACK_LEDGER.md) retains all 58 report dispositions until they are reconciled against the exact processed replacement build.

P0 means loss/leakage, cross-account access, broken sign-in, unrecoverable posting, or failed deletion. P1 blocks a core task. P2 blocks professional clarity, accessibility, or a visible failure state. P3 is minor polish. A suspected issue becomes an accepted defect only after reproduction or direct source proof; unknown test coverage is a gate, not a claimed failure.

## Sharing reviews

1. **Mugshot export:** capture its preview, what will be visible, who can see it, where it can be sent, recipient access, invalid/expired link behavior, and private-field exclusion. Compare Fitbod for clarity only when examples are available; do not assume its layout or audience model is the specification.
2. **Google Maps import:** invoke Mugshot from the iOS Share sheet; test URL/text clue extraction, Apple Maps match and mismatch, no result, network failure, selected Want to Try/list/note, queued receipt, app reopen, account mismatch, retry, and final Saved/list state. The queue receipt and final-save confirmation must refer to different stages.

## App Review and release gate

Check the exact release package against the [current App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/): stable/complete behavior and reviewer access; user-content filtering, reporting, blocking and contact; privacy disclosures and permission alternatives; in-app account deletion. Verify working reviewer credentials and support/privacy URLs, current App Store text/screenshots, iPhone-only metadata, app/extension/widget signing and capabilities. Historical 0.5.1 submission text is not a current asset. Document each check with build, owner and evidence before submission.

**Go only when** the exact candidate has zero open P0–P2 findings, required backend contracts and preservation checks pass, the first-run and return-user matrices pass on a supported minimum-iOS device and current iOS, and reviewer access works. Then seek a separately authorized staged TestFlight handoff under [the upload policy](TESTFLIGHT_UPLOAD_HANDOFF.md), accept the processed build, reconcile all feedback, and only then prepare App Store submission. Archive, upload, group assignment and submission are separate actions and have not occurred in this audit.
